INDIA QUANT 2.1 - LOCAL CONDITIONAL TRADE RESEARCH

1. Extract to a NEW folder. Preserve the previous app and journal.
2. Install Python 3.11 64-bit and the Python Launcher.
3. Double-click START_WINDOWS.bat; visit http://127.0.0.1:8765.
4. Trade Tips opens automatically. Start with Delivery and the "New to trading?" guide.
5. Choose Delivery, Intraday, Swing, Calls & puts, or Futures. No watchlist is needed.
6. The default scan is Nifty 500. You can choose Nifty 200/100/50, all regular NSE stocks,
   or all F&O stocks and indices. Large first scans can take several minutes.
   Results appear every ten stocks. Coverage shows missing data; Stop ends after a batch.
7. Read the entry, stop and target on a matching setup. WAIT means no entry matches yet.
   Each loaded stock has a small price graph; See chart opens its completed candles.
8. Calls & puts and Futures show a listed contract and underlying direction.
   Without a broker connection, underlying levels are not premium/futures entry prices.
   Authorized broker quotes can unlock current-session contract levels when checks pass.
9. Open a plan, recheck current prices and use your own broker for any manual trade.
   The app does not send an order. Advanced research tools are in the sidebar menu.

The downloaded directory is provider-backed. Search is not limited to the 25-instrument
watchlist. No profitable pretrained model, never-fail filter or real order execution.
F&O direction ideas use technical rules, not a validated option-profit forecast.

Synthetic SIM-* data is fictional. It is not a live quote or market-performance test.
Do not expose this local single-user service to the public internet.
For local distribution run: python build_local_package.py
Before charging for recommendations or redistributing data, resolve the applicable registration
and data rights. Read docs/COMMERCIAL_ROADMAP.md, TEST_REPORT.md and PRODUCTION_GATES.md.
