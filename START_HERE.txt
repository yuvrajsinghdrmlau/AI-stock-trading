INDIA QUANT 2.1 - LOCAL CONDITIONAL TRADE RESEARCH

1. Extract to a NEW folder. Preserve the previous app and journal.
2. Install Python 3.11 64-bit and the Python Launcher.
3. Double-click START_WINDOWS.bat; visit http://127.0.0.1:8765.
4. Market Explorer loads an empty or older catalogue automatically. Refresh it when stale.
5. No Upstox key? Choose Public historical candles or import your CSV on a stock's chart.
   Authorized broker quotes remain optional: set your own token in .env and restart.
6. Add stocks to your watchlist. In Daily Trade Desk choose Swing or Delivery, then
   Refresh data + find setups. Review CONDITIONAL BUY, WAIT, BLOCKED and EXPIRED results.
7. Open Review plan / open broker to inspect entry conditions, source, expiry and levels.
   Stop and target references must be recalculated from the actual fill. Confirm the session,
   current price and company events on your broker. Intraday plans need authorized live data.
8. Copy/save a research record and open your own broker to place any order manually.
   The app does not send an order. Train/review models in AI strategy lab for measured evidence.

The downloaded directory is provider-backed. Search is not limited to the 25-instrument
watchlist. No profitable pretrained model, never-fail filter or real order execution.
F&O is read-only chain/payoff research, not a validated options recommendation model.

Synthetic SIM-* data is fictional. It is not a live quote or market-performance test.
Do not expose this local single-user service to the public internet.
For local distribution run: python build_local_package.py
Before charging for recommendations or redistributing data, resolve the applicable registration
and data rights. Read docs/COMMERCIAL_ROADMAP.md, TEST_REPORT.md and PRODUCTION_GATES.md.
